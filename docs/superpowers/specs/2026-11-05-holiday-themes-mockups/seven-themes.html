The seven main themes: picture, title font and colors

Each strip is the TV's title area in that holiday's soft colors; the two bars under it show the panel color and border. The startup animation is described below each one (it plays once when the app opens, then stops). Click any you like as-is; tell me in the terminal what to change on the others.
